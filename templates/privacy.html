Official Municipal Policy

Privacy Policy & Citizen Data Protection

Effective Date: 1st January 2026 • Compliance: Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA 2016) • Jurisdiction: Sindh, Pakistan

1. Point-in-Time Geolocation Telemetry

GPS telemetry is collected strictly at the single point in time when an incident report is submitted. CivicPulse AI has zero background location tracking and never logs or tracks civilian movement. Coordinates are clustered into a 50-meter radius to detect duplicate complaints and minimize redundant work orders.

2. Automated Civilian Privacy Masking

All photographic evidence uploaded to CivicPulse AI undergoes automated edge computer vision pre-processing. Bystander civilian faces and vehicle license plates are detected and permanently blurred prior to long-term storage. Hardware EXIF metadata (camera serial numbers, device model) is completely stripped.

3. Citizen Mobile Number Protection (SMS Only)

Citizen mobile phone numbers (Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone, SCOM) are collected strictly for transmitting official SMS status receipts and field crew dispatch notifications. Phone numbers are securely stored, never sold, rented, or disclosed to commercial advertising networks.

4. Ephemeral Voice Note Processing

Urdu and Sindhi audio voice notes are processed through OpenAI Whisper and Gemini multimodal speech models to extract actionable infrastructure notes. Raw audio files are stored securely in local media directories and automatically purged upon ticket validation.

5. Statutory Rights & Ombudsman Access

Under the Sindh Local Government Act 2013 and PECA 2016, citizens retain the statutory right to request verification, rectification, or deletion of their contact details once a civic ticket reaches Resolved status. Inquiries may be directed to the Provincial Municipal Ombudsman at privacy@civicpulse.sindh.gov.pk.
